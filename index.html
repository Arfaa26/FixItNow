How FixItNow works

A real end-to-end flow: authenticate, diagnose, save the result, and revisit it later.

01 · INPUT

Describe + upload

Choose the device, type the symptom, and optionally attach a JPG, PNG, WEBP, or GIF photo.

02 · ANALYZE

Gemini AI or safety rules

With a Gemini API key, the server can analyze the symptom and image. Without one, the built-in safety-first engine still works.

03 · SAVE

Build your repair history

Every authenticated diagnosis is stored in SQLite with its result and image, so users can review or delete it later.

Run a diagnosis

Sign in to save your diagnosis. The app works without an external AI key, and becomes image-capable when GEMINI_API_KEY is configured.
