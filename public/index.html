        Before you start

        A few details make the diagnosis much more useful — and help you avoid unsafe troubleshooting.

        🎯

        Say when it happens

        For example: only during spin, only after heating, or every time you turn it on.

        👂

        Describe the clue

        Grinding, clicking, humming, burning smell, leaking water, flickering, error codes — these clues matter.

        📸

        Photograph evidence

        Show visible damage, leaks, error displays, labels, or the affected area in good light.

        🛑

        Know when to stop

        Smoke, sparks, shocks, gas, swollen batteries, or exposed live wiring are technician territory.
